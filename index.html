How the dollar signs work

Each verified tier uses the median of five distinct, representative, full-price clothing styles in USD, before tax and shipping. A tier describes a dated sample, not every item a brand sells. Sale prices, accessories and limited collaborations are excluded.

131 brands have verified samples. Twelve show “Price under review” and are excluded from dollar-tier filters until a comparable sample is available.
